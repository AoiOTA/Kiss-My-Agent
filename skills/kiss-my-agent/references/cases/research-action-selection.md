# Research action selection: concrete repairs

These cases come from Re:Nav development observations and the associated delivery failure. They illustrate decisions, not a general performance claim or proof that KMA caused the errors. Project-specific thresholds, scenarios, metrics, and permissions remain in the project.

| What happened | Choose the next action | Supported conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An adapter excluded legal revisits, repeated observations, and recovery after collisions; tests preserved those exclusions. | Restore the native legal choices and correct the tests. Give estimates and past collisions to the deciding agent as evidence. Check one actual entry before expanding the comparison. | A shared restriction does not prove a valid experiment. Restoring capability repairs the research object; it does not yet prove a method gain. |
| A suggested inspection was not a selectable action, or an executed inspection image was overwritten by the parent stage's last image. | Trace actual request → delivered choices/input → selection → execution → feedback → next action, and repair the first lost handoff. | Field presence, saved images, and internal execution do not prove actual consumer receipt or behavioral effect. |
| Complete R2R tasks succeeded, but the target conditional mechanisms were `NOT_TRIGGERED`. | Distinguish unreachable mechanism, no relevant opportunity, and autonomous non-use. Repair the consumer, choose a legally relevant scenario, or retain non-use. Do not force calls or repeat unchanged runs to wait for evidence. | Task success is supported; mechanism benefit is untested in these trajectories. |
| A successful arm used more time and distance than a failed arm. | Report success according to the user's metric priority, then the extra complete cost, and separately bound causal attribution. | Success is a positive task result with a cost tradeoff. Faster failure is not improved completion efficiency; one system success does not identify a component effect. |
| A frozen comparison later exposed a capability or evaluator defect that invalidated its conditions. | Stop the affected run, keep the failed/invalid record and incurred cost, repair the owner, and freeze a separate valid comparison. | Invalid conditions leave the product hypothesis unresolved. An intact experiment's negative outcome instead supplies valid negative evidence. |
| A generated Three.js page passed a temporary preview, but its server exited before the user opened it. | Complete access through an appropriate existing host or usable self-contained delivery, verify the actual entry, and state its access lifetime. | File creation and a past preview prove those steps; delivery completes only when the user can use the result as requested. |

Judge the next ordinary task by its actual decisions and usable outcome. User-reminded recovery is corrected execution. Guide loading, new fields, tests, and delegation are not measurements of proactive effectiveness or efficiency; a benefit claim needs a comparable baseline and the relevant costs.
